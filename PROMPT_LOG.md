Profile/Mini-Portfolio website for my university coursework.

My name is Ian Mwashanja. I am interested in software engineering,
programming, web development and learning how to build useful software
solutions. I am currently developing my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript,
databases, C# and Flutter.

My goal is to create a short and natural About Me section for my
personal portfolio website.

Please write an About Me section that:
- Uses simple and natural English.
- Sounds like a university student, not a professional company.
- Is written in first person.
- Mentions my interest in software engineering and web development.
- Mentions that I am learning and improving my technical skills.
- Does not exaggerate my experience or claim skills I do not have.
- Does not use complicated vocabulary.
- Is approximately 2 short paragraphs.

Format the response as plain paragraphs that I can edit and include
in the About Me section of my HTML page.
